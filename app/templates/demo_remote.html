Lines to land

  • Community early warning, not crime reporting
  • An aggregation tool, not a reporting tool
  • No public map, no public list, no enforcement power
  • Desk sees day-level dates only, never who sent it
  • Every escalation is a person's decision, on the audit log
  • Referral brief is the bridge to Amotekun, a state outfit
  • Claude reads the text; the word lists stay as a floor and a fallback
  • The accusation check and thresholds are fixed rules, not a model

If Ushahidi comes up

  • They publish a public map. This refuses to publish
  • They collect reports. This aggregates signals too weak to report
  • They take text as given. This strips identity first
